About ShoeMakeri

ShoeMakeri is a shoe website that will permit users to buy shoes and other sporting items[shirts, socks, etx.]. When you enter the URL shoemakeri.com for the website you will see a wide range of shoes and designs. You will see all the tabs(links) on top of the screen. The links will be "brands", "men", "women", "shop all", "sneakers". There will also be a sign-in however that is optional. If you do sign-in, you would then be able to save past designs, and the website will remember your shoe size. The men link will take you to the men items on the site. The ladies link will take you to the ladies item on the site. The sneaker link will show all items for both men and women from new to old on the site. The Brand link will take you to a page that show the rundown menu of brands like Nike, Adidas, and other name brands.
